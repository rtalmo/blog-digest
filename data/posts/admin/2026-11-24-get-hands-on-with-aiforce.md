- AIforce is presented as a single platform where humans and agents work from any interface (Claude, Slack, WhatsApp, Teams) while keeping the user's Salesforce setup.
- The post lays out a four-step learning path: try AIforce as a user, configure it as an admin, join a hands-on workshop, then roll it out to your org.
- As a user, you can try the out-of-the-box Salesforce Skills and Connectors in Claude, including standard Deals skills for reviewing deals, spotting risks, updating opportunities and planning next steps, via the 'AIforce in Action' YouTube playlist.
- The admin layer covers MCPs, Skills, Plug-ins and the Headless Toolkit, with the 'Get Started with AIforce' Trailhead trail and the 'Explore Agentforce Coworker' quick start badge, which uses a developer edition org to enable Agentforce Coworker and assign permissions.
- The open-source Pronto Sample App, built on the Headless Toolkit, demonstrates agentic experiences and the Headless Experience Layer (HXL). An 'Introduction to AIforce' hands-on workshop in a developer edition org was announced for December, and Trailblazer Relations also hosts free monthly 'Get Hands-on with AIforce' workshops.
