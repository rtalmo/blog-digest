- Dreamforce 2026 runs September 15-17 in San Francisco and on Salesforce+, with 1,600+ breakout sessions, 150+ hands-on trainings and demos, 50+ keynotes, and 240+ community roundtables.
- Key admin events include the Admin Keynote ('Bring the Agentic Enterprise to Life'), the Main Keynote with Marc Benioff, the 'True to the Core' Q&A with Parker Harris, and Sneak Peeks of unreleased features across Agentforce, Headless, Flow, and User Access Management.
- Hands-on areas include Headless 360 Basecamp (a 15-minute exercise configuring Salesforce MCPs in a third-party AI system), Camp Mini Hack (30-minute challenges on Agentforce, Apex & Flow, and Data 360), Camp Trailhead badges, and the Agentforce Zone (Agentforce Studio, Builder, Operations, Voice, Multi-Agent Orchestration, Observability).
- Recommended admin sessions cover Agentforce Coworker, Setup with Agentforce for multi-step tasks, securing headless Salesforce implementations, connecting ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude to a live org through Headless 360, Data 360 basics, admin security fundamentals, and screen flows.
- FeatureFest offers a first look at Winter '27 features, including IdeaExchange favorites. Trailblazer Bootcamp (Sept 12-14) offers certification prep, and completing the Road to Dreamforce Quest by Sept 14 enters you in a draw for 1 of 100 prize boxes.
