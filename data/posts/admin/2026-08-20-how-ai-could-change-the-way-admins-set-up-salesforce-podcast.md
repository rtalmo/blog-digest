- The Salesforce Admins Podcast interviews Khushwant Singh, SVP of Product Management for the AI Application Development Platform, about Headless 360, which was announced at TDX.
- Headless 360 separates the frontend from the backend. Salesforce data, visualizations and agents can then appear in external sites, apps, Slack, ChatGPT and other surfaces without rebuilding them. One example is Williams Sonoma's Olive agent, an Agentforce agent embedded on a non-Salesforce site.
- Salesforce is translating the platform into model context protocols (MCPs), skills and metadata so AI agents can use it directly. The layers were built for humans over the past 27 years and are now being opened to agents as well.
- Setup with Agentforce already lets admins use natural language prompts to create objects, Lightning pages and flows, manage users and access, and troubleshoot formulas. It is presented as the first step toward setting up Salesforce through conversation.
- Admins stay in control. Humans initiate actions, validate results and provide governance, and Singh stresses that trust, quality and governance matter more as AI adoption speeds up. He advises admins to try Setup with Agentforce now.
