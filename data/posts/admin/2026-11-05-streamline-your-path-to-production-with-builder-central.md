- Builder Central is a new no-code beta experience that builds and deploys apps and agents from plain-language prompts in a single hub.
- It already knows your existing orgs' configuration and security/permissions, so new apps and agents inherit those controls.
- Unlike Setup with Agentforce, which makes direct changes such as creating a field, Builder Central targets complex builds like full data models, flows, and agents.
- It complements rather than replaces Flow Builder, Lightning App Builder, and Agentforce Builder, and deploys through Lifecycle Center following your pipeline.
- Safety features include interactive metadata visualizations (data model, agents, flows, pages, permission sets) and ephemeral preview orgs, which are temporary sandbox clones, so nothing touches sandbox or production until you publish.
