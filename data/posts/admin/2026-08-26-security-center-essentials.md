- Security Center Essentials is a free, centralized org security dashboard for admins that is generally available now and needs no support case to enable.
- The dashboard shows a Health Check Score measured against the Salesforce baseline or a custom baseline you define.
- It keeps 30 days of metric history so you can track improvements and regressions, and use it as evidence of progress for leadership.
- Core metrics shown in one place include connected apps, installed packages, login IP ranges, and trusted IP ranges.
- To enable it, assign the 'View Security Center Pages' permission, then open it from the App Launcher or Setup.
