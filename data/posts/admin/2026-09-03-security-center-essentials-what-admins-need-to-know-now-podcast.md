- Security Center Essentials is now fully rolled out to production orgs and some sandboxes, extending Health Check's point-in-time configuration score with ongoing visibility into an org's security posture.
- It currently surfaces seven metrics, including installed managed packages, connected apps and third-party access, trusted IP ranges, who installed what, and recent changes; this is planned to expand to 14 metrics by Dreamforce.
- Sabrina Simeroth recommends two review questions: what should be in my environment (owner, installer, users of each package), and what changed most recently (expected or not, by whom, and the impact on business processes).
- Admins should set a monitoring cadence tied to business milestones, such as checking before and after a weekly Tuesday update, and escalate anything unexpected to the security and governance team.
- A perfect Health Check score shouldn't be the goal: it should start a security review rather than end one, and any gaps you accept should be known, intentional risks.
