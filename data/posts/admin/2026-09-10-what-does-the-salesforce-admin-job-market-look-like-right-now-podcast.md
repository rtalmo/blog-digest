- The Salesforce Admins Podcast episode features recruiters Maria Alvarez-Zarrilli (Throughline HR) and William Walsh (Harvey Nash) on the current admin job market.
- The market is more competitive than a few years ago, and certifications alone no longer differentiate candidates; employers want admins who have solved real business problems in Salesforce.
- Maria's top hiring signal is 'requirements literacy': pushing back, making recommendations, and keeping a long-term vision, since AI is shortening project runways.
- William says demand is growing for skills in data quality, automation, AI tools, and specific industry clouds (e.g., Revenue Cloud and CPQ in financial services), while warning against becoming too niche.
- Candidates should describe impact rather than features on resumes and in interviews, and should attend user groups and local events, since community referrals often beat mass-applying.
