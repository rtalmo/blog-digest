- The September Monthly Retro podcast episode with Joshua Birk and Kate Lessard recaps Dreamforce 2026 from an admin's perspective.
- Builder Central is a low-code/no-code AI workspace for building React sites, flows, agents, data models and permissions from plain-language prompts, with automated testing, safety checks and built-in controls.
- Admins can upload requirements documents and business-process information to Builder Central so it builds against their actual data model and org structure; the hosts stress testing in a sandbox and "trust but verify".
- Headless 360 and the Headless Experience Layer let Salesforce surface in Slack, Claude and other interfaces; the keynote demoed running an auto-launched case-escalation flow directly from Claude.
- The main Dreamforce takeaway is that governance and trust matter more, so admins should focus on data cleanup and strict permissions, and on tracking how data moves across platforms and who can access it; new tools include Security Center Essentials and the Transaction Security Policy Accelerator.
