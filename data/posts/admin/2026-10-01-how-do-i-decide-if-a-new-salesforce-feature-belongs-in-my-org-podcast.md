- Salesforce Admins Podcast guest Alastair Dinning, a Senior Solution Architect with Salesforce Professional Services, shares a framework for deciding whether a new release feature belongs in your org.
- He recommends reading release notes with your business processes and user pain points in mind, so you can tell real value from a shiny new toy.
- To surface pain points, he suggests sitting with key users, running a steering committee of power users, or mining a case management system for feedback.
- He describes three paths for a new feature: build it declaratively (tailored but costs time), buy it from the AppExchange (costs money but deploys fast, and partners may make improvements), or code it custom (most expensive and least flexible).
- He suggests building a declarative prototype that gets about 80% of the way there to collect feedback before investing in custom code, and notes that sometimes keeping the org stable is the best choice.
