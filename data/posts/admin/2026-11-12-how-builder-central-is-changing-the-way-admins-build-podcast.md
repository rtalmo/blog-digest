- Builder Central, now in beta, lets admins go from describing an idea to a working no-code solution on the platform using agent-powered tools.
- It lets you build and preview work in a virtual environment before publishing to a sandbox, so you avoid making and rolling back metadata changes while iterating.
- Visualizers show how a flow you are building will look in your org before it is deployed.
- Because the agent has access to your org metadata, it can help refactor or test existing flows and reduce tech debt, and can refresh existing apps with a modern UI and brand colors.
- Everything built follows your org's security settings and naming conventions, and the guests say work that used to take days or weeks can take hours.
