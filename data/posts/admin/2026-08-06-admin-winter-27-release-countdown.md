- The post is an admin countdown of key dates for the Winter '27 Salesforce release.
- August 13: admins can sign up for a pre-release Developer Edition org with all Winter '27 features, and anyone with a Summer '26 pre-release org can log back into it.
- August 19: Release Notes are published in Salesforce Help, and the Release Readiness Trailblazer Community Group offers community breakdowns and Q&A.
- August 27 before 5 p.m. PT is the deadline to refresh a sandbox that is not slated for preview, using the Sandbox Preview Guide, and sandbox preview begins August 28 (the post also mentions a January 8 deadline, which looks inconsistent with the August dates).
- Winter '27 reaches production on September 4, October 2 and October 9, depending on the instance, so check the Maintenance Calendar and Release Updates in Setup, and look for Admin Evangelist Jen's Be Release Ready blog on October 5.
