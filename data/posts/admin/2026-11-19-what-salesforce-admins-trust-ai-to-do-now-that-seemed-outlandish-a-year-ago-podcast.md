- In a Salesforce Admins Podcast November retro, evangelists Jennifer Lee, Kate Lessard and Joshua Birk describe how much more they trust AI agents than they did a year ago.
- Kate now delegates work to AI through a headless toolkit and keeps direction and judgment, but no longer executes every step herself.
- Jen first set Claude to show her each action for approval when building demos. She later let it run on its own, including creating and loading dummy data and running tests, though she still has to tweak the results.
- Josh uses a personal assistant agent across email, Slack and calendar to reclaim his inbox, and has one AI proof another AI's work. It can send emails and texts only with his review, and it has no access to credit cards or bookings.
- Josh's test for how much access to give an agent is how quickly you can recover if it goes wrong: a mistaken email is easy to fix, while an emptied bank account is not.
