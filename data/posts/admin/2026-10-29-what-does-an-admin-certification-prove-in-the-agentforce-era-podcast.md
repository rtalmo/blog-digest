- Salesforce Admins Podcast episode with Stacy Jacobs (Partner Ecosystem & GTM Leader) argues certifications still matter even though AI can walk anyone through building things like a custom object.
- Stacy says getting the most from Agentforce requires understanding how Salesforce works in your specific business context, since AI assembles the pieces but humans decide what the finished solution should look like.
- Certifications help even veteran admins learn best practices for security models, native out-of-the-box features they may not be using, and how to build confidently with AI.
- Many Salesforce certifications emphasize soft skills that help you build the right solution to the actual business problem, rather than just an app that meets stated requirements.
- Stacy recommends using AI to gather study materials and prepare for exams, including Slack skills that help you study, but not to obtain answers to exam questions; Trailhead links include Salesforce Certified Business Analyst and AI Associate.
