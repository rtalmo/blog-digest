- Builder Central is a new no-code, AI-powered workspace for building apps, agents, and experiences from natural language. It enters beta the week after Dreamforce for orgs with Agentforce enabled, and every org gets it free with usage limits.
- Salesforce Quick Pages lets admins and permissioned users build single-page React apps on top of existing data without putting org metadata at risk.
- Agentforce Coworker is an out-of-the-box autonomous assistant in Lightning that respects the logged-in user's permissions. Admins can set it up in under 5 minutes.
- The Headless Experience Layer (HXL) lets admins build deterministic UI widgets tied to data, automation, and business logic that work across every interface. The HXL Playground is available to try now.
- Security Center Essentials is a free dashboard for security posture, enabled by assigning the View Security Center Pages permission, with no support case needed.
