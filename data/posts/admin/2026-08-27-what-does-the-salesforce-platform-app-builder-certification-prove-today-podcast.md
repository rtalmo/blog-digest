- The Salesforce Certified Platform App Builder exam has been refreshed in its 11th year, based on interviews with subject matter experts including admins, app builders, and architects.
- The biggest change is a deeper emphasis on Flow: choosing the right flow type for a situation, creating flows, troubleshooting them, and maintaining and monitoring them.
- Agentforce is now included as an automation tool in app building, but only at the level of knowing when to reach for it, since the separate Agentforce Specialist exam covers deeper expertise.
- Salesforce fundamentals and user interface remain exam topics because they have stayed important since the exam's early versions, and Salesforce Admin was named the role that most aligns with the exam.
- Prep advice includes using the new Trailhead trail with interactive sandbox badges, reading the exam guide closely (verbs like 'create' or 'configure' signal deeper knowledge than 'describe' or 'understand'), and scheduling the free Dreamforce certification exam.
