- The author, a former Marketing Cloud admin at an online travel agency, says earning the Salesforce Administrator certification gave them the platform foundation to push back on data models and build cross-functional solutions.
- Certification is framed as resting on three pillars: validated core competency, governance and trust standards (security, compliance, ethical implementation), and problem-solving frameworks for turning business requirements into technical solutions.
- The post argues that domain expertise is what makes AI output reliable rather than merely plausible, and that human oversight is needed where AI models fail on governance.
- Because AI lets almost anyone produce decent baseline work, the author says a Platform Admin certification gives hiring managers independent verification of hands-on skills.
- Keeping a certification current is presented as a way to keep skills from calcifying around one org's way of doing things, and the post links Trailhead resources including AI Certification and AI Fundamentals.
