- Winter '27: Angular is GA, joining React as the second generally available Multi-Framework UI framework.
- Microfrontend is GA: embed React and Angular in Lightning Experience, Experience Cloud, the Salesforce mobile app and Mobile Publisher.
- Non-site (internal) Multi-Framework apps can now be distributed as managed packages.
- Adds localization/i18n and MFA support; Multi-Framework Insights (performance and usage) is in Beta.
