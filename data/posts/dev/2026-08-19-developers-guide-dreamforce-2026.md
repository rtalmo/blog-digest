- The guide highlights Dreamforce 2026 (San Francisco and Salesforce+ livestream) with 1,600+ breakout sessions, 150+ hands-on trainings and demos, 50+ keynotes, and 240+ community roundtables.
- Key keynotes for developers are the Main Keynote with Marc Benioff, the Developer Keynote 'Build the Agentic Enterprise' (Headless 360 demos), and the unscripted 'True to the Core' with Parker Harris.
- Trailblazer Forest hands-on areas include Headless 360 Theater (six 20-minute intro sessions), AIforce (a 15-minute exercise configuring Salesforce MCPs in a third-party AI system), Camp Mini Hack (30-minute challenges), Camp Trailhead badges, and Platform Park (20 demos).
- Featured developer sessions cover the Metadata Grounding MCP and Skills Library with Agentforce Vibes and Claude Code, deterministic agents with Agent Script, querying Data 360 from Apex in five lines, Document AI with Headless Data 360, Apex best practices for apps and agents, and Mobile SDK Skills.
- The Road to Dreamforce Quest must be completed by September 14, 2026 for a chance at 1 of 100 prize boxes; Trailblazer Bootcamp runs September 12-14, and Dreamfest is September 16 at Oracle Park.
